Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Auto Video Editor is a desktop application for locally editing video clips and preparing short-form videos for supported platforms. Some platform integrations are still in development or testing.

Information accessed

If a user connects an account using an official platform authorization flow, the application may access basic profile details allowed by the user, such as a profile identifier, display name, or avatar. Additional access is requested only for features the user enables and only where authorized by the platform.

Local processing and storage

Video clips selected for editing are processed on the user's computer. The application may store selected video files, generated clips, publication metadata, and platform authorization credentials locally. The application does not automatically publish content without a user-approved publishing action.

Third-party services

When users choose to authorize a platform or send a video to it, data needed for that action may be exchanged with the relevant platform under that platform's own privacy terms.

Choices and retention

Users can delete local project and output files. Where the feature is available, disconnecting an account removes locally stored authorization credentials. Users may also revoke authorization within the relevant platform's security settings. Disconnecting locally does not necessarily revoke permission at the platform.

Updates

This policy may be updated as features change.
